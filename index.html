Who We Are

Serving Uganda's Most Vulnerable

KICYYSO is a registered NGO in Nansana, Wakiso District. We uplift orphaned children, disadvantaged youth, and vulnerable families through practical support rooted in Islamic values.

  • Orphan sponsorship and daily welfare support
  • Education — school fees, books & uniforms
  • Healthcare and food assistance for families
  • Islamic education and youth empowerment

Our Story

A Calling Born from Compassion

KICYYSO was founded in response to the growing number of orphaned and economically disadvantaged children in Uganda. A group of community members came together under Islamic principles of mercy and solidarity to ensure no child is left behind.

From humble beginnings — distributing food and paying a handful of school fees — we have grown into a registered NGO touching hundreds of lives annually. By the grace of Allah, we are only getting started.

Our Purpose

Mission, Vision & Values

🎯 Our Mission

To uplift vulnerable children, youth, and communities through education, welfare support, Islamic values, and sustainable development — ensuring every child has a fair chance at a dignified life.

🌅 Our Vision

A Uganda where every child has access to opportunities, dignity, and hope — where no child goes to bed hungry, uneducated, or unloved.
