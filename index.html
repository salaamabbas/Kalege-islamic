Who We Are

Serving Uganda's Most Vulnerable

KICYYSO is a registered NGO in Nansana, Wakiso District. We uplift orphaned children, disadvantaged youth, and vulnerable families through practical support rooted in Islamic values.

  • Orphan sponsorship and daily welfare support
  • Education — school fees, books & uniforms
  • Healthcare and food assistance for families
  • Islamic education and youth empowerment

Our Programmes

How We Help

Six core programmes creating lasting change for children and families across Uganda.

🤲

Orphan Support

Comprehensive care for orphaned children — food, shelter, clothing, and the warmth of community.

📚

Education

School fees, books, uniforms, and supplies so every child can access quality education.

🏥

Healthcare

Medical check-ups, treatment, and essential medicine for vulnerable children and mothers.

🕌

Islamic Education

Qur'an classes, Islamic studies, and spiritual mentorship for children and youth.

🎓

Youth Empowerment

Vocational training and skills development giving young people a sustainable future.

🍲

Food & Welfare

Food packages and essential welfare supplies for Uganda's most vulnerable households.

Orphan Support

No Orphan Should Face Life Alone

Thousands of children in Uganda have lost one or both parents to illness, poverty, and hardship. KICYYSO ensures no orphan faces their journey without food, education, and community.
